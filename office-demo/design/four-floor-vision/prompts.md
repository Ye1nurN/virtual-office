# Промпты концептов четырёх этажей

Режим: встроенный image_gen, по одному независимому вызову на изображение.

Оба изображения-референса приложены к каждому вызову: план пользователя и выбранный визуальный стиль.

## Первое показанное изображение — рабочий этаж

Use case: ui-mockup / stylized-concept. Create a realistic production-quality visual design concept for a browser based virtual office SaaS for one company, expandable to many companies, pilot 20–100 employees. This is a concept image, not a screenshot of existing implementation. Two supplied images: Image 1 is the authoritative FOUR-FLOOR spatial planning reference, Image 2 is the authoritative ART AND UI STYLE reference. Adapt the planning of Image 1 and retain the sophisticated 3D pixel/voxel appearance of Image 2: steep top-down orthographic camera, dimensional voxel/chibi people, detailed wooden furniture, blue-grey tiled floors, soft teal/green seating, abundant carefully placed plants, glass walls, cream floating UI with Inter-like Cyrillic typography, warm clear DAYLIGHT through large windows. It must look like a rich actual 3D game environment, not flat pixel sprites, not a Minecraft blockout, not photorealistic corporate architecture. Maintain connected clear walking routes and sensible doors. Four floors all have top-left/top-right stairs, compact service strip, a lift near top centre, coffee/kitchen to the upper right. Floor 1 guest reception/open lobby centrally, two guest meeting rooms on left, event auditorium on right. Floor 2 central business teams islands, marketing and sales left, customer success/support in central zone, two meeting rooms and focus room right. Floor 3 central product/design/developer islands, QA and IT left, a small infrastructure room left, meeting and lounge right. Floor 4 director/finance/HR left, shared strategy and learning rooms centrally, legal right, mentoring lounge lower right. Toilet/service spaces only subtle compact scenery, no product functions assigned to them. Floors indicate function not seniority: career progression is personal skill criteria, not gaining access to a superior floor. Illustrative people only. No keystroke surveillance, productivity scores, employee rankings, automatic promotion claims. If dates used, anchor 2026-09-26; preferably omit dates. Match clean Russian labels with readable type, no paragraphs of tiny text. Focused coherent composition, no gratuitous charts, metric cards, noisy badges, nested panels or browser/device chrome. Preserve hard constraints and create one independent concept per image, no variants collage.
Concept direction name: «Рабочий этаж». Target dimensions: 1440 × 1024 desktop application. ONE immersive screen, showing ONLY the complete third floor at a steep top-down angle, retaining reference 1's third-floor silhouette and placement. Full-bleed daytime voxel 3D office dominates 85 percent of screen. At top rear compact service/stair strip with lift labelled «Лифт» near centre and kitchen top-right. Left edge: QA room upper-left, small server/infrastructure room mid-left, IT room lower-left. Centre: FOUR clearly separated four-person desk islands for product teams, with blue-grey carpet, oak desks, plant dividers, one collaborative design table and a modest plant seating spot. No excessive rows of desks. Right edge: glass meeting room at upper right and lounge at lower right. Wide connected paths; low cutaway front walls. Warm daylight is plentiful, natural shadows, no night lighting. Front entry centre. Render furniture, screens, desk lamps and expressive voxel employees at the same detailed quality as style reference 2. Camera sufficiently high to read whole plan. Preserve top floating UI: «Наш офис», tabs «Офис», «Команда», «Развитие», search «Найти человека или отдел». New compact floating floor switch on LEFT vertical rail with only four clear rows: «4 Управление», «3 Продукт» selected mint, «2 Бизнес», «1 Гости». Rail occupies a narrow margin, no obstruction of important rooms. In-world dark slate room labels: «Тестирование», «Разработка», «Дизайн», «ИТ-поддержка», «Переговорная». Selected own avatar turquoise ring and label «Вы · Фокус». Bottom centred cream communications dock like reference 2 with small avatar, «Алексей», «Фокус», mic/camera/chat icons; lower right zoom 100%. Only one small white contextual tooltip near department board: «Разработка» / «Создаём и развиваем продукт» / button «Открыть отдел». No large sidebar, career dashboard or extra UI cards. This direction prioritises everyday walking, colleague contact and quick floor switching.
